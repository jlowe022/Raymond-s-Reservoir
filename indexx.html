Raymond's Reservoir

This is your space. Come back to it whenever you need some pouring into.

res·er·voir /ˈrezərvwär/ noun

A large natural or artificial lake used as a source of water supply. A place where something, especially a fluid, is kept in store. A reserve or supply of something.

A reservoir doesn't just hold water — it holds it for a purpose. It stores what is needed so that when the dry season comes, when the ground is parched and the well runs low, there is still something to draw from. It is a place of abundance built for the moments of lack.

That is what this is for you, Raymond. This is your reservoir. Scriptures, letters, prayers, and truth about who you are — all stored here, waiting for you to draw from it. In your dry seasons and your full ones. When you need reminding, when you need strength, when you just need to be poured into.

Come back whenever you need a drink.
